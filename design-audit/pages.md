# Boki — Full Route & Screen Inventory

Generated by scanning every `*.routes.ts` file in `apps/frontend-web/src/app` plus every
in-page modal/drawer and multi-step flow (which don't have their own route, but are
distinct screens for design purposes). "Needs login" describes the actual guard behavior,
not just presence/absence of a guard.

Legend: ✅ Yes · 🚫 No · 🔓 Guest-only (blocks *logged-in* users) · 🟡 Optional (page loads either way, content differs)

---
